Industrial partner: [ASELSAN Inc.](https://www.aselsan.com/en)

Tools: COMSOL Multiphysics, MATLAB.

This work covered electromagnetic launcher component design, armature optimization, and pulse power supply system analysis. It included coupled electromagnetic and thermal finite element models, improved test-simulation agreement, and investigation of velocity skin effect and lamination modeling.
