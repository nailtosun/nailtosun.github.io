Research Focus

  • Electrical drives, power electronics, and electrical machine design for systems ranging from grid-scale generators to ultra-high-speed miniature machines and pulsed-power electromagnetic launchers.
  • PhD research on current source inverter drive solutions for ultra-high-speed electrical machines, including GaN and bidirectional GaN prototype development, sensorless motor-drive algorithms, and modulation methods.
  • Additional interests include wide-bandgap devices, finite element analysis, electromagnetic launchers, power quality, control-system design, optimization, and renewable generation forecasting.
