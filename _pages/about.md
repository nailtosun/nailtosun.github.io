I am a PhD candidate at Newcastle University in the [Sustainable Electrical Propulsion CDT](https://research.ncl.ac.uk/electric-propulsion/), working with Dyson on current source inverter drive solutions for ultra-high-speed electrical machines. My research focuses on power electronics, electrical drives, wide-bandgap devices, machine design, finite element analysis, and experimental validation of high-speed motor-drive systems.

Before joining Newcastle, I worked as a research engineer at [METU PowerLab](https://odtu.github.io/) on electromagnetic launchers, pulsed-power systems, generator refurbishment, and renewable generation forecasting. My work spans systems from grid-scale hydro generators to 100 krpm machines and high-current pulsed-power electromagnetic launchers.

For recent publications, see my [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=2_zTqOwAAAAJ&hl=en), [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Nail-Tosun), or the publications page.
